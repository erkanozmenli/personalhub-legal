PersonalHub – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Overview

PersonalHub is a mobile application designed to help you organize your reminders, documents, and places.

We value your privacy. PersonalHub is designed to store your personal data locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect

PersonalHub does not collect your personal information or transmit it to servers operated by the developer.

The application:

  • Does not require you to create an account.
  • Does not display advertisements.
  • Does not use analytics or user behavior tracking services.
  • Does not use third-party crash reporting services.

3. Local Data Storage

Reminders, documents, places, and attachments that you create in PersonalHub are stored locally on your device.

The developer does not have access to this data.

4. Permissions and Notifications

PersonalHub may request permission to send local notifications for your reminders.

The application may also access files you select when you initiate actions such as attaching documents or importing backup files.

5. Backup and Restore

PersonalHub allows you to export your data and restore it later.

Backup operations are initiated by you. You choose where exported backup files are stored or which services you use to share them.

Exported backup files are not encrypted. We recommend storing backup files securely and avoiding sharing them with unauthorized individuals.

If you store backup files in iCloud Drive or another third-party cloud service, that provider's privacy policy may apply.

6. Data Sharing

PersonalHub does not sell your personal data or automatically share it with third parties.

Your data may leave your device only through file-sharing or backup operations that you explicitly initiate.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains stored on your device until you delete it or remove the application.

Device backups created by your operating system and backup files you have exported may remain available after you remove the application.

8. Children's Privacy

PersonalHub is a general-purpose application and is not specifically directed at children.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Changes will be published on this page, and the last updated date will be revised accordingly.

10. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how PersonalHub handles your data, please contact us at:
